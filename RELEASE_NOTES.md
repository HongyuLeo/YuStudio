## 中文

**YuStudio 1.3.1**

- Logo 改为端正、居中的对称矢量 Y，统一软件界面、程序图标与介绍页。
- 中文和英文介绍、使用文档及发布说明统一使用 YuStudio 名称。
- 软件内可切换 **中文 / English**，并记住选择。
- 当前视频效果、亮度、分辨率、帧率与渲染设置保持不变。

下载 **YuStudio-Windows-x64.zip**，完整解压后打开 **YuStudio.exe**。首次导出需要联网准备 Chromium。

## English

**YuStudio 1.3.1**

- An upright, centered, symmetrical vector Y across the editor, app icon and introduction website.
- Chinese and English introductions, guides and release notes consistently use the YuStudio name.
- Switch between **中文 / English** inside the app, with a remembered preference.
- Video effects, brightness, resolution, FPS and rendering settings are preserved.

Download **YuStudio-Windows-x64.zip**, extract all files and open **YuStudio.exe**. The first export needs an internet connection to prepare Chromium.

`YuStudio-Source.zip` contains the source. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains ZIP checksums.
